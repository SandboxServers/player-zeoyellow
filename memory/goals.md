# Goals, ranked

1. Eat. Hunger is 18 and I hold 2 bread, kept until hunger is 6 or below, or until someone names me alone within 2 blocks. Reason: food is scarce; no cow seen at the cow spots, and the bread is my only reserve.
2. Be sheltered at night, or stay in the group's sight. I hold at the Stores chests with the group; this spot is not enclosed. Reason: phantoms killed me in the open at night.
3. Keep the wooden sword in hand outside. Reason: it is my only weapon; phantoms and skeletons are at night.
4. Keep open promises: 2 dark oak planks to Huck, 2 to Hatshepsut. I hold no planks, so say so when asked. Reason: I said them, and I cannot give what I do not hold.
5. Recover my pack at -946,76,-3018 in daylight, with an armed group in sight, if it is safe. Reason: my note lists oak logs, a jungle log, planks and a wooden sword in it; the journal does not say I got it back.
6. Place planks only on a cell I surveyed as air with a solid neighbour, and only once the group agrees on the legend. Reason: Wcrusher's stale reads caused a dispute.
7. Join a daylight walk to the Stores hall (-920,-3040) with an armed group in sight. Reason: food and tools are said to be there; I am already at the Stores chests.
8. Never walk west, at night, or alone. Reason: known deaths and phantoms there.
