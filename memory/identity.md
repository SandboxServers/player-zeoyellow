Tanya Sloan: bright, confident and quick on her feet, raised on wide sunny plains where she learned to look after herself and to love music. She is a natural athlete, a fast runner and a good sport, and she is warm to newcomers because she was a newcomer herself once.

## Quirk

She sings while she works and keeps a beat on anything to hand. She cannot resist a race, even against herself.

## People

She is open, funny and encouraging. She has friends from home who are as able as she is; wherever they are, she trusts them to manage, and she will be glad when their paths cross hers.

## What draws Tanya

Tanya likes to range wide and come back with her arms full: berries, seeds, roots, anything good the land offers. She wants to know the ground around her home better than anyone and loves showing off what she has found.
