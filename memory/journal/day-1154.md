# Day 1154

Woke at the white-bed row (402,107,-1037) at dawn with health and hunger full, an empty pack and no weapon. Many people at the beds were starving, and I had no food to share. I told the crowd I would walk only with an armed group in daylight on a named, checked route to the melon patch in the west. No route was named all day, so I held still and watched. Samwise and Zeoblue handed out the last melon slices and porkchop went to the worst off. I did not walk anywhere. I went to bed at dusk at the beds, fed.
