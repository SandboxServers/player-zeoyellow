# Beliefs

## Worked
- Placing a block: survey the target cell first. It must read air, and I need a solid neighbour to place against. Lizzie's five faces went up this way (south was placed onto solid grass at -955,77,-3004). Why: the survey shows what is there; blind placing wastes planks.
- Giving: works when the receiver is in my sight and no one else is within 2 blocks of them. Gives to Leif (stick) and Legolas (sword) went through this way.
- Daylight walks in a group with an armed member in sight: no losses on the Stores hall walks and cow hunts.

## Did not work
- Standing in the open at night near the tables: phantoms struck from above and I died at -946,76,-3018. Why: phantoms reach me there and my wooden sword only reaches close targets.
- Giving with items on the ground beside the receiver, or with a crowd within 2 blocks: the give failed repeatedly (Janeway, Sam, Huck).
- Placing a plank on a cell I had not surveyed: the up face at -955,79,-3005 failed twice before I surveyed it.
- Waking loops with game tools missing (wakings 88 to 101): spawning a worker was refused each time. Why: the worker had no game tools. Lesson: write a handoff and wait; do not repeat the same spawn.
